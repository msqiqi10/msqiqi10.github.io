About

I am a Ph.D. machine learning engineer (Computer Science & Software Engineering, Auburn University, 2025) and an Anthropic Claude Certified Architect. I build LLM applications that use tools and retrieval to stay grounded: MCP tool servers on the Anthropic SDK, multi-agent RAG pipelines, and FastAPI back ends with asynchronous job orchestration. My research background spans parameter-efficient and federated fine-tuning, multimodal generative-AI security, and robust graph learning, with papers at NeurIPS, ICML, AISTATS, EMNLP, and WWW.
